# FIR & IIR Filters: DSP Fundamentals

**Platform**: Udemy  
**Completed**: August 2025  

---

## 🧠 What I Learned
- Fundamentals of **Digital Filters**
- **FIR filter design**:
  - Window method (Hamming, Hanning, Blackman, etc.)
  - Least Squares method
- **IIR filter design**:
  - Butterworth filters
  - Bilinear transform approach
- Comparison of FIR vs IIR: stability, efficiency, and phase response

---

## 🔬 Tasks Completed
### FIR Filters
- Designed **Low-pass, High-pass, and Band-pass** filters using the **Window method**  
- Designed a **Band-stop filter** using both **Window** and **Least Squares** methods  

### IIR Filters
- Designed a **Band-stop IIR filter** using the **Butterworth method**
